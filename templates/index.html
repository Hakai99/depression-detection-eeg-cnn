Analyze an EEG Recording How It Works

From recording to result in three steps

The pipeline follows the same signal processing used in EEG-based depression research: filter, segment, classify.

STEP 1

Upload

Provide a .edf recording covering at least 15 of the 19 standard electrodes, sampled at 256 Hz.

STEP 2

Process

The signal is band-pass filtered (1–40 Hz), normalized per channel, and split into 4-second overlapping windows.

STEP 3

Classify

A lightweight EEGNet-style CNN scores every window and returns an averaged confidence for Healthy vs. MDD.

Note: This is an academic prototype for a final year project. It is not a certified diagnostic tool and should not replace professional clinical assessment.
